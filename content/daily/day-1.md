* **Headline:** 5 Questions Smart Angels Ask Before Writing a Check
* **Format:** Single Image / Carousel Cover
* **Slide 1:** "5 Questions Smart Angels Ask Before Writing a Check (Save this for your next pitch review)"
* **Slide 2:** **1. "What is your Net Revenue Retention (NRR)?"**  
  *Why it matters:* If NRR is <100%, you are filling a leaky bucket. If NRR is >120%, the business grows even if sales stops today.
* **Slide 3:** **2. "How much of your growth is organic vs paid?"**  
  *Why it matters:* Blended CAC hides burning unit economics. Ask for pure paid CAC and payback period in months.
* **Slide 4:** **3. "Who owns the cap table, and what is dead equity?"**  
  *Why it matters:* If inactive former co-founders or predatory early incubators own >20%, future Series A VCs will pass.
* **Slide 5:** **4. "What is the single reason this startup dies in 18 months?"**  
  *Why it matters:* Tests founder self-awareness. Great founders know their kill-risks immediately.
* **Slide 6:** **5. "Does this deal fit my deterministic thesis?"**  
  *Why it matters:* Check size, stage, and sector mismatch ruins angel portfolios. Know your criteria upfront.
