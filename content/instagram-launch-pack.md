### 4.2 Slide-by-Slide Art Direction & Copywriting

#### Slide 1: Cover Hook

* **Slide Copy:**
  * **Main Title:** 5 Red Flags in Early-Stage Startup Pitches.
  * **Subtitle:** (What Smart Angels Check Before Writing a Check)
  * **Footer Callout:** Save this for your next angel diligence review 💾 ➔

---

#### Slide 2: Red Flag 1 — The TAM Hallucination
* **Visual Art Direction:**
  * Contrast box:
    * ❌ Bad: "The global healthcare market is $12 Trillion, we only need 0.1%!"
    * ✅ Good: Bottom-up SOM calculation based on customer count × ACV.
* **Slide Copy:**
  * **Header:** 01. The TAM Hallucination
  * **The Red Flag:** Founders claiming a multi-trillion-dollar Total Addressable Market by quoting a generic Gartner report, concluding: *"If we just capture 1%, we’re a unicorn."*
  * **What Smart Angels Look For:** Bottom-Up SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market). 
  * How many specific buyers exist at your exact Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), and what is their verified Annual Contract Value (ACV)? If a founder can't name the first 500 customers, the TAM is fiction.

---

#### Slide 3: Red Flag 2 — The Blended CAC Mirage
* **Visual Art Direction:**
  * Graph showing customer acquisition cost: A deceptive flat line labeled "Blended CAC" vs. an escalating dotted curve labeled "Paid CAC."
* **Slide Copy:**
  * **Header:** 02. The Blended CAC Mirage
  * **The Red Flag:** Reporting a low "Blended Customer Acquisition Cost" by lumping organic word-of-mouth with paid ad spend.
  * **Why It’s Dangerous:** Blended metrics hide unit economic decay. When paid spend scales from $10k to $100k/mo, organic referral volume doesn't scale linearly. Paid CAC often triples.
  * **The Diligence Question:** *"What is your fully-loaded, paid-only CAC per acquisition channel, including agency fees and software overhead?"*

---

#### Slide 4: Red Flag 3 — Cap Table Dead Weight
* **Visual Art Direction:**
  * Pie chart illustration comparing a healthy early cap table (Active founders 75%+) vs a toxic cap table (Inactive advisor 20%, predatory incubator 15%, active founder left with 40%).
* **Slide Copy:**
  * **Header:** 03. Dead Equity on the Cap Table
  * **The Red Flag:** 20%+ of common stock held by non-operational co-founders, early advisors who gave 2 hours of advice, or predatory early incubators.
  * **Why It Matters:** Future institutional venture investors (Series A/B) will refuse to fund a startup where the active executive team does not have enough equity incentive to survive 7–10 years of grueling execution.
  * **The Check:** Always inspect the fully-diluted cap table before signing a term sheet.

---

#### Slide 5: Red Flag 4 — "LOI Theater" & Soft Commitments
* **Visual Art Direction:**
  * Document icon stamped with `NON-BINDING` in faint red, vs a bank statement icon stamped with `CASH RECEIVED` in green.
* **Slide Copy:**
  * **Header:** 04. "LOI Theater" vs. True Traction
  * **The Red Flag:** A slide boasting: *"We have $2.4M in pipeline LOIs (Letters of Intent) signed!"*
  * **The Reality:** Non-binding LOIs cost corporate executives zero dollars to sign and are abandoned 85% of the time.
  * **What Smart Angels Check:**
    * Are these binding pilot contracts with upfront deposits?
    * Is there active software usage or recurring pilot fees?
    * Has a customer ever fought through procurement to keep the tool?

---

#### Slide 6: Red Flag 5 — The "We Have No Competitors" Delusion
* **Visual Art Direction:**
  * A 2x2 competitive matrix where the startup is magically isolated in the top-right corner with zero competitors around it.
* **Slide Copy:**

  * **The Truth:** A lack of competition doesn't mean you've discovered a secret goldmine—it usually means:
    1. The market is too small to sustain a business.
    2. Five well-funded companies tried and failed.
    3. The competitor isn't a direct startup, but Excel, email, or human inertia.
